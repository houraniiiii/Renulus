The exact MiMo/DeepSeek tokenizer configuration pages at the repository
revisions above were inaccessible through the browsing tool. That is a retrieval
limitation, not proof of absent files. The immutable artifact bytes/digests and
runtime behavior therefore remain unverified. No remote code was executed.

The next substantive evidence is a provider-qualified mapping of immutable
tokenizer plus framing/tool/image rules to each selected route, or an explicitly
adjudicated counting service available within that selected subscription. It
must satisfy existing InputCounter/DeploymentEvidence semantics, retain owned
counter finalizers, and remain separate from credential-revision-bound account
capacity/capability observations. Public model context lengths or successful
answers cannot supply those account observations.
