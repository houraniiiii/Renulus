Current `runtime/renulus/memory/service.py:205–227,346–358` separates the awaited coroutine from extraction-thread completion. Upstream also separates [interruption acceptance from settlement](https://github.com/anomalyco/opencode/blob/4b6fea2875e4f2e3e8a8fee181803e54f7056845/packages/core/src/session/execution.ts#L23-L39).
