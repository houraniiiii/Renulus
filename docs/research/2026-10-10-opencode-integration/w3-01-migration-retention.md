**Receipt lifetime: concrete remaining gap.** Static inspection of [command/draft/delete](current-source-map.md#source-09) at `76cfdff` shows saved draft text remains in earlier command results after owner deletion; [backup inclusion](current-source-map.md#source-13) includes `conversation_commands`. Thus deleting the owner does not establish payload erasure. Edit existing command ledgers/replay and owner deletion: live receipts resolve canonical results by reference; deletion scrubs result text and payload digests, retaining an opaque operation-ID/terminal-unavailable tombstone for the profile's lifetime. Do not expire deduplication identities while backups can replay them. Return unavailable for retired operations; never rerun their effects. Extend typed restore to preserve these tombstones without preserving deleted payloads.
