Research and access date: **2026-10-04**. Planning only; no curriculum, provider,
framework or content acquisition is selected by this note. Round 2 subsequently

**Confirmed user choices.** Renulus serves EU practising nephrologists throughout
specialisation: topic learning, cases, examinations, programme support and current
updates, in short daytime sessions or focused active study. English comes first;
other EU languages are optional later. Chat/Explain and Test are separate. Scored
questions come from a reviewed bank; generated questions support practice.
Important learning information should persist without duplication. Interactive
daily-practice case discussion is educational. The user and assistant review and
validate; an external panel is not a prerequisite, and assistant review cannot
guarantee accuracy. The final application is Windows only: an open-source harness
using existing models, without retraining or owner-hosted services/inference.
Prefer simple, maintained backend components; none is selected here.

**Engineer-owned recommendation: separate programme and exam maps.** Keep EU as
the audience geography. Offer ESENeph as a candidate examination track and national
programme mappings as separate, country-specific work. Its linked UK curriculum
should not silently become every EU learner's programme. Represent objectives,
cases and bank items with shared topic identifiers, then attach programme/exam
mappings and their editions. Do not present question performance as evidence of
procedural competence or completion of specialist training.

For a concrete starting blueprint, the linked Federation document allocates
30/200 questions to glomerular/interstitial disease, 26 to acute injury and
fluid/electrolyte/acid–base problems, and 24 to CKD/urinary abnormalities. Other
domains include cardiovascular disease, inherited disease, urology, dialysis,
transplantation and cross-cutting care. Authoring batches should cover varied
domains instead of concentrating the product on one subject or a few clusters.
No item count or completed-bank claim has been adopted. Full product
breadth remains learning, cases, examinations, programme support and updates across
nephrology. Authoring and implementation can proceed in stages while tracking
coverage gaps against general topics and the selected ESENeph track.

**Engineer-owned recommendation: bounded content access.** Start with manually
curated titles, canonical URLs, editions, publication dates, document type and
last-checked dates. Link to the ERA manual, English CKD takeaways and KDIGO
guidelines in the user's browser. Review official publication pages monthly;
KDIGO Direct is an optional user subscription, not a reason to read their inbox.
No supported content API was verified in this shortlist. Show “last checked”
instead of claiming continuous currency; deduplicate updates by document and
edition. Keep drafts, final guidance and commentaries visibly distinct.

Full ingestion remains unapproved for these third-party materials. Check exact
item terms separately for storage, indexing, model transmission, adaptation and
redistribution before enabling those operations. Begin with original Renulus
explanations and questions carrying source locators and an explicitly chosen
content licence. Do not copy publisher quizzes, presume future question-set
delivery, or treat private correspondence as a permission grant.

**Engineer-owned recommendation: bank lifecycle and scores.** The user-plus-assistant
review team is settled; review throughput and cadence are later operational details.
The assistant can draft original synthetic vignettes and challenge distractors; the user checks
the source passages, answer and rationale before release. Record author,
reviewer, objective, source edition/locator, review date, item version and status:
draft, reviewed, published, withdrawn. Review includes why each alternative is
inferior and whether the stem has one defensible answer. Corrections create a
new version; retain the attempt's original version and notify affected learners
when a key changes. Withdraw ambiguous items from future tests; flag affected
historical scores instead of silently rewriting them.

Score published items deterministically against their reviewed keys. Keep generated
practice outside scored aggregates and never promote it automatically. Reserve
test item families from Chat retrieval and practice generation; exposure to an
item or close variant marks later attempts as repeats. Report first attempts and
repeats separately. An open-source distributed bank cannot promise secret items
or examination-grade security. Link editable learning notes to concepts and
merge redundant copies while preserving distinct examples, qualifications and
learning episodes. Whether learning memories are suggested or saved automatically
remains a product decision.

**Daily-practice discussion after round 2.** Support text, summaries, PDFs and
images. Extract relevant text and preserve source/page references; distinguish
extracted text from image interpretation and expose uncertain extraction for
correction. Structured prompts can capture timeline, relevant findings,
units and the learner's question. Ask for missing information,
explore reasoning and save learning points. Retention, now accepted in round 3:
default raw case text and attachments to the current
session; an explicit Save retains it locally with visible deletion/retention
controls. Keep learner memory separate from raw cases. Before the first remote
model send, show the destination and exactly what context leaves Windows;
provider retention remains provider-specific. A local session deletion must not
claim deletion at a provider. Source-update requests should contain no case text.

**Round 3 decisions — now accepted.**

Uploaded study documents can form a reusable personal library. Learning memory
is automatic and editable; raw cases/attachments require explicit Save.
Evidence lookup is automatic when needed, with citations, dates and failure
states. See the [current decision queue](../../planning/DECISION_QUEUE.md).
Selecting particular national mappings can follow when useful.
