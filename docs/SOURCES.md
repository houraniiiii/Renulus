# Sources and use boundaries

Renulus retains a small reference shelf, selected brand assets and reviewed
development tooling. The bibliography identifies candidate public sources and
the dates recorded in inherited notes. It does not establish an acquired corpus,
current clinical recommendations or permission to process or redistribute source
content.

## Content-use status

Source-specific use eligibility remains **review-needed**. Public visibility,
membership access and institutional access should not be recorded as permission
for every product operation. Keep retrieval, display, caching, indexing or
embedding, model input, derivation, evaluation, training and redistribution
distinct. Training is not part of the confirmed Renulus direction.

Useful provenance vocabulary includes source identity and owner, canonical link
or publication identifier, document edition, publication and revision dates,
recorded access date, source status, exact passage or figure locator, applicable
licence or permission reference, attribution and unresolved use restrictions.
This is reference vocabulary, not an implemented registry or selected schema.

Preserve the distinction between final guidance, drafts, preprints, commentaries
and educational resources. A dated source observation is not a verified current
status. A citation or source locator does not by itself establish that a claim
is supported.

## Retained material

- [Reference shelf](../references/README.md): two concise notes and separately
  documented taxonomy labels. The notes retain their original evidence cutoffs
  and public bibliography links.
- [Brand record](../assets/brand/README.md): selected logo assets and their
  creation/export provenance. Generated artwork is not evidence of clinical accuracy.
- [Design tools](DESIGN_TOOLS.md): development packages and applicable software
  licences/notices. A tool licence does not clear third-party medical illustrations
  or educational content.

Private correspondence, patient records, credentials, native/account state,
unclassified datasets and restricted source or question packs stay outside this
public repository. Cleanup grants no access to those materials. Do not publish
private agreements as evidence of permission; retain a public-safe use status
without exposing the agreement.

The original implementation and complete research notes remain in the separate
archive described by [the workspace guide](WORKSPACE.md). Bring in only a
reviewed subset when a later authorised task needs it, preserving provenance
and applicable rights.
