## Scope and method

Checked official topic/edition pages, publisher identities and available update routes with the web reader; searched title/DOI-specific notices and followed official records. Ordinary unauthenticated HTML requests supplemented accessible UKKA, EAU, OUP and NICE metadata. No credentials, private material, paid APIs, models, stored source originals or restricted PDF redistribution were involved. Access challenges and 403 responses were not bypassed; a public CKD PDF request returned 403 and was not retried through a fallback.

A supplementary **22-work** [NLM ESearch/EFetch][nlm-api] notice query combined work-specific title/topic and baseline year in Title fields with Published Erratum, Retraction of Publication, Retracted Publication or Expression of Concern publication types. Publication-date range ended **2026-10-05**. UKKA's non-journal hyperkalaemia revision used its own route. Queries were bounded discovery, not completeness checks: they missed separately identified hyponatraemia and urine-eosinophil corrections. **No row certifies absence of retraction.** Publisher/Crossmark closure remains unproved where stated.

**Final-listed** means the newest final identified on the retrieved official topic/catalogue view for that scope. It does not establish that all recommendations remain current, that linked bytes incorporate every correction, or that a failed check can refresh verified-current status. A newer upload directory is not an edition date. Month/year-only dates retain that precision; no publication day is invented. Expansion IDs retain their distinct scopes, although they share publications.
