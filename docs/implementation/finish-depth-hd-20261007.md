## Clinical and objective boundaries requiring parent attention

1. **Register reconciliation:** BC Renal is absent from the current register.
   `register_id: G09` is an explicitly **proposed, unregistered allocation** in its
   snapshot, not a claim that G09 is approved. Parent must assign/register an
   appropriate identity before publication; do not misattribute BC guidance to
   UKKA G02. This lane leaves docs/SOURCES untouched.
2. **Exact objectives:** all proposals nominate T20.O02, “Plan modality, access
   and ongoing dialysis care with the person's preferences,” for its ongoing-care
   safety component. Case debriefs include patient communication and comfort.
   This is partial support, not complete preference-sensitive modality/access
   planning. T20.O01 is initiation; T23.O01 is “Explain solute clearance, fluid
   removal and modality constraints.” Neither honestly names acute emergency
   recognition/response. No forced credit is supplied. Parent must accept the
   partial T20.O02 mapping or explicitly adopt an emergency objective.
3. **Blood return:** air/haemolysis no-return and a contaminated disconnected
   circuit are distinct rules. NDT's general bleeding/return discussion must not
   override the specific contamination scenario. No automatic return decision
   is taught for every uncontaminated bleed, arrest or other emergency.
4. **Excluded techniques:** positioning, syringe air aspiration, hyperbaric
   referral, transfusion thresholds/route and drug regimens are not assessed.
   BC's positioning/aspiration steps were seen but not generalized to every
   air-embolism presentation. Its neighbouring anaphylaxis/blood-return and
   rupture algorithms were not adopted. The selected source locators must not
   be treated as endorsement of every adjacent recommendation.
5. **Remaining D29/D28/D30:** the wider intradialytic emergency bank,
   anaphylaxis/disequilibrium/arrest pathways, access rupture, full home-HD and
   maintenance prescription, water-system operations and procedural competence
   remain outside this slice. Hypotension is credited to existing1.3 evidence.
   Parent acceptance, integrated case access and reserved assessment eligibility
   remain necessary; no global currency or curriculum cell is cleared here.
