# Education and citation evidence

Retained during cleanup: 2026-10-04. Original research cutoff: **2026-10-01**.
The summaries and publication dates below are preserved from eligible public
research notes; sources were not rechecked and experiments were not reproduced
during cleanup. No study evaluates Renulus.

## Selected original studies

| Study and publication date | Observation retained from the notes; limit |
| --- | --- |
| [Medical student reliance on artificial intelligence in nephrology education](https://academic.oup.com/joneph/advance-article/doi/10.1093/joneph/aajag033/8735677), *Journal of Nephrology*, 2026-07-16 | AI advice was associated with both helpful and harmful answer changes and a modest net accuracy increase. This is not a durable-learning or Renulus-effectiveness result. |
| [Large Language Model Influence on Diagnostic Reasoning: A Randomized Clinical Trial](https://jamanetwork.com/journals/jamanetworkopen/fullarticle/2825395), *JAMA Network Open*, 2024-10-28 | LLM access did not significantly improve the measured diagnostic-reasoning score over conventional resources alone. This physician trial does not establish that all learning interfaces or newer models fail. |

The findings concern mathematics, not medical education. |
| [To Trust or to Think: Cognitive Forcing Functions Can Reduce Overreliance on AI in AI-assisted Decision-making](https://arxiv.org/abs/2102.09692), *Proceedings of the ACM on Human-Computer Interaction*, 2021; manuscript posted 2021-02-18 | Interventions reduced overreliance with an acceptance/usability cost in a nonmedical task. Educational transfer remains unestablished. |

## Citation and evaluation distinctions

[Enabling Large Language Models to Generate Text with Citations](https://aclanthology.org/2023.emnlp-main.398/),
*EMNLP*, December 2023, separates answer correctness from citation quality.
Its general-domain results are not nephrology performance evidence.

The inherited notes distinguish software checks, source-location verification,
claim support, applicability and educational outcomes. A working citation link,
another model's agreement or a synthetic software test does not establish them
all. These distinctions are retained as evidence terminology, without selecting
an evaluation protocol or learning interaction.
