## Useful vocabulary

- **Use operations:** discovery, retrieval, display, cache, extract/index/embed,
  model input, derive, evaluate/train and redistribute. Permission for one
  operation does not establish permission for the others; unknowns stay review-needed.
- **Source identity:** stable identifier, owner/publisher, canonical URL or
  DOI/PMID, title, edition and language/translation provenance.
- **Version and status:** final, draft, preprint, commentary or education;
  publication, revision and access dates recorded separately; correction,
  retraction and supersession links.
- **Evidence locator:** section or recommendation identifier, page/paragraph,
  table/figure identity, headers, units, footnotes and applicable population.
- **Use evidence:** exact licence or permission reference, permitted operations,
  attribution, third-party exclusions, expiry and explicit unresolved scope.

These are portable terms, not a product schema or legal clearance. The source
inventories described metadata and acquisition research, rather than a verified
full-text teaching corpus. Their historical approval labels are not current
permission records.
