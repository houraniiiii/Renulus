# Retained references

This is a small reference shelf extracted during cleanup. It preserves eligible
public-source links and useful terminology from inherited notes, without copying
full notebooks, competitor surveys or their proposed product designs.

- [Source-use notes](source-use-notes.md): operation and provenance vocabulary,
  source/asset policy links and the original 2026-10-01–03 evidence cutoffs.
- [Education evidence](education-evidence.md): selected original studies and
  citation-evaluation distinctions recorded in notes with a 2026-10-01 cutoff.
- [Taxonomy labels](taxonomy/README.md): separately retained labels and their
  provenance; no validated curriculum or society endorsement is implied.

No external sources were rechecked during this cleanup. Recorded access dates
are historical observations, not publication dates or current verification.
These references do not select a framework, interface, curriculum, model or
roadmap, and they establish no Renulus effectiveness result.

Use remains subject to [source boundaries](../docs/SOURCES.md). The complete
inherited notes remain in the separate archive described by
[the workspace guide](../docs/WORKSPACE.md).
